# 📡 Wavecon Telecom | 5G Launch Impact Analysis

### Did the 5G launch improve business performance?

A Power BI analysis of Wavecon Telecom's performance before and after its 5G launch, looking at revenue, customer activity, ARPU, regional trends, and plan performance.

## 📊 The Business Story in Numbers

| KPI           | Before 5G |  After 5G |     Change |
| ------------- | --------: | --------: | ---------: |
| Total Revenue | ₹15.98 Bn | ₹15.90 Bn |   🔻 0.50% |
| ARPU          |    ₹190.2 |    ₹211.3 | 🟢 +11.05% |
| TAU           |  Baseline |         — |   🔻 8.28% |

*Figures reflect the reported analysis. TAU is shown as a percentage change because the absolute values are not included here.*

## 🔍 What Did the Analysis Reveal?

**1. Higher ARPU, but lower overall revenue**

Average revenue per user increased by 11.05%, yet total revenue fell slightly. Higher revenue per user alone was not enough to drive overall revenue growth.

**2. Subscriber activity needs attention**

The reported TAU KPI declined by 8.28%. This makes customer activity and retention important areas for further investigation.

**3. Performance varied by city**

Delhi, Chennai, and Ahmedabad showed larger revenue declines, while Lucknow, Gurgaon, and Patna performed comparatively better after the launch.

**4. Some telecom plans need a closer look**

Plan-level analysis helps identify offerings that may be losing revenue. Before discontinuing a plan, its customer adoption, usage, and contribution to revenue should be reviewed.

## 💡 What Would I Recommend?

* **Investigate subscriber losses:** Understand whether customers reduced usage, changed plans, or became inactive.
* **Review underperforming plans:** Check revenue trends and customer behaviour before changing pricing or discontinuing an offering.
* **Take a city-specific approach:** Investigate weaker markets separately and learn from better-performing regions.
* **Track the right KPIs together:** Monitor revenue, ARPU, and subscriber activity to avoid relying on a single metric.

**The key finding: better ARPU did not automatically mean better overall business performance.**

The next step is to understand subscriber activity, investigate weaker markets, and review underperforming plans before making business decisions.
